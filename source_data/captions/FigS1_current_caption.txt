Fig. S1 | Draw-level performance of the five fixed-budget corpus designs. a, Top-1 accuracy for represented-location queries (G1). b, Top-1 accuracy for unseen-location queries (G2). c, G2 Top-3 accuracy. d, G2 within-draw median Euclidean distance from the highest-ranked template location to the true leak location. All panels use H0 with no additional template deletion and projection-residual retrieval. Bars show the mean and black caps ±1 sample s.d. across the 15 corpus/query-location draws (the values of Table S3b); the white circles on each bar are the individual draws, spread horizontally for visibility. Bar fill grades the design means within each panel from blue (lowest) to red (highest; reversed in d, where a shorter distance is better) and carries no further information. Each design contains 540 templates, and each query group contains 180 queries per draw. A′ (30 × 6*, hatched) shares A's locations but excludes all query magnitudes. A single Gaussian perturbation vector with component standard deviation 0.02 m is reused across all queries within each draw. The horizontal axis is categorical and does not represent a fitted spatial design curve. Distances are reported in network-coordinate units; conversion to physical metres has not been established.
