Fig. 3 | Network partitions and pressure sensors. a, EXA7: 381 junctions, 15 partitions and 30 sensors. b, City D: 383 physical junctions, 15 partitions and 28 sensors. The outlined region is enlarged below to resolve its dense sensor neighbourhood; it is a second view of the same model. c, Haining: 920 junctions, 15 partitions and 62 sensors. Coloured regions are Voronoi cells merged by graph-partition label and provide a visual aid; they do not define the partitions geometrically. Grey lines and dots are hydraulic links and junctions, vermilion circles are sensors, and open squares are reservoirs or tanks. All junctions and sensors are retained in the full-network views. Each panel preserves the aspect ratio of its network-file coordinates; panels and the inset use different scales. EXA7 uses the fixed sensor set in Methods. City D and Haining use the seeded greedy coherence-minimizing placement procedure in Supplementary Note 1.8. The displayed network models, partition labels and sensor assignments are those used in the evaluations.
