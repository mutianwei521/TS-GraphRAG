Fig. 2 | From hydraulic simulations to partition-level retrieval. a, Configure the network. The reduced network retains its topology; three shifted nodal traces illustrate pressure levels, not similarities. Time-averaged baseline pressures assign physical links wuv(0) = (p̄u + p̄v)/2, followed by global min–max scaling with floor 0.001; a zero range leaves weights unchanged. Leiden uses local moves, refinement and aggregation to optimize the configuration-model objective. The saved EXA7 configuration fixes 15 partitions and 30 sensors, two per partition. Sensor selection used a demand-response heuristic, without demonstrated optimality. b, Encode a scenario. The reference grid combines 30 leak locations, nominal magnitudes of 2, 5, 10, 20, 35 and 50 L s−1, and demand multipliers 0.7, 1.0 and 1.2, yielding 540 templates. Multipliers apply throughout each 24 h simulation, not to successive time segments. Each prescribed-demand leak is paired with a no-leak simulation in the same demand state. Signed differences of mean pressures are encoded in fixed 30-sensor order: absolute differences ≤0.01 m become zero; retained values are rounded to four decimals. c, Store the numerical dictionary. The example record is P0, node 258, 10 L s−1, demand ×1.0. The complete 540 × 30 matrix is read top-to-bottom within each 90-row block, then left-to-right across six blocks. Thin outlines enclose fingerprints; source row and sensor-column orders are preserved. Shared linear colour shows signed components divided by each row's L2 norm, from −1 to 0. All components are nonpositive; 18 zero-norm rows remain zero. Normalization is for display only. Optional graph/text resources do not prune primary retrieval. d, Retrieve and rank. For eligible nonzero templates, an unconstrained aj = fjTy/(fjTfj) gives orthogonal residual rj = ‖y − ajfj‖2; zero-norm templates receive infinite residuals. The query strip and signed stem profile reuse the stored fingerprint at node 258 in P0 (10 L s−1, demand ×1.2), without channel interpolation. Four template strips are actual P0–P3 records. The independent two-channel teaching example is not a reduction of this query. Filled templates overlay blue query outlines at common scale, height = −Δp; hatching marks absolute per-channel fitted residuals. Labels give Euclidean mismatch: A, 2.20→0.13 m; B, 1.27→1.27 m. This illustrates fitting, without establishing hydraulic invariance. Ascending template residuals yield distinct-zone ranks by keeping each label's first occurrence. Benchmark masks control eligibility before matching; graph fusion and LLM re-ranking are separate comparisons (Fig. 6). Network layouts, a–b traces and two-channel rankings are schematic; matrix, strips and the 30-channel profile use the unchanged simulated corpus. All evaluated evidence is simulation-based.
