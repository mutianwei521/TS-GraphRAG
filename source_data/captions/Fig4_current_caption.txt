Fig. 4 | Localization under explicit holdout protocols. In every panel, coloured bars show means, fine grey whiskers show ±1 sample s.d., and open circles show all five stochastic repetitions. Categorical offsets separate overlapping circles without changing their measured-value coordinates; circle sizes are reduced only where needed to fit the repetitions within each bar's lane and do not encode another variable. Hatching identifies projection-residual retrieval. a, Top-1 partition accuracy of all eight methods on 540 reference queries under H0–H8 (Supplementary Table S2b). The dotted line marks chance accuracy, 1/15. b, All eleven methods on the fixed 90-query subset, ordered by H2 mean accuracy. Paler and full-colour bars show H0 and H2, respectively, as indicated by the two-tone key. The projection row is highlighted (Supplementary Table S2a). c, The same comparison for seven methods on those 90 queries under H0, H2, H3, H4 and H8, with method colours shared with a and b. d, Projection-residual prediction destinations under H0, H2 and H8: correct predictions (blue), adjacent errors (peach) and other errors (coral). Adjacent errors are predictions in a partition sharing a pipe with the true partition; the denominator is all 540 predictions per run. Each repetition regenerated query noise and refitted stochastic models while retaining the corpus, sensors, query identities and exclusions (seeds 41, 581, 1121, 1661 and 2201). All 690 displayed observations, including coincident values, are retained in the Source Data. H0 deletes nothing; H1 the exact scenario; H2 location; H3 magnitude; H4 demand state; H5–H7 unions of location/magnitude, location/state and magnitude/state; and H8 all three factors. The reference corpus is unscreened and all evidence is simulated. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
