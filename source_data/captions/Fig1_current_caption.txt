Fig. 1 | Protocol-aware, simulation-grounded leak localization. a, Global non-revenue water1 and urban physical losses2 are literature context, not study measurements. The challenge combines sparse sensing, variable discharge and demand, and partition-level output. b, EXA7 has 381 junctions (J), 30 fixed sensors (S, red) and 15 graph partitions (Z, node colours). The library contains 540 templates (30 locations × 6 discharge rates × 3 demand states). Demand waves are symbolic. The complete 540 × 30 matrix displays absolute fingerprint components normalized by each row’s L2 norm, with a shared square-root colour transfer; 18 zero-response rows remain zero. The illustrative query y uses a stored P0 template scaled by 0.8 plus Gaussian channel noise (s.d. 0.02 m; seed 3); its horizontal coordinate is sensor order, not time. For nonzero signed fingerprints, aj = fjTy/(fjTfj) and rj = ‖y − ajfj‖₂. Rank bars show the three smallest template residuals, all from P0; the network highlights this illustrative output. The grey branch summarizes separate comparisons (Fig. 6): strict H8 fusion versus residual means over five noise realizations (43.0% versus 59.7%); the historical single-realization graph-variant versus cosine 1-NN comparison across five protocols; and stratified estimates for language-model re-ranking under H2. c, Red indicates deletions: H0, none; H1, exact scenario; H2, location; H3, discharge; H4, demand state; H8, the union of these three factors; H9, true-partition templates, a label-support audit. Deletion glyphs are schematic, not counts. Points and pale columns show the same mean Top-1 accuracy; caps give ±1 sample s.d. over five repetitions on 540 fixed queries. d, Mean Top-1 on the matched 90-query subset over five repetitions; H8 combines location (L), discharge (Q) and demand (D) exclusions. e, G2 unseen-location means ±1 sample s.d. over 15 corpus/query-location draws, each with 180 queries; all four designs retain three demand states and 540 templates. Among the four displayed allocations, A has the highest represented-location mean and C90 the highest unseen-location mean; this is not a universal optimum (A′ is included in Fig. 5). The 15 dots indicate only C90 > A in all 15 G2 draws. All experimental evidence is simulated. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
