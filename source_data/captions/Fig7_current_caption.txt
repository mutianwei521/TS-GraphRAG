Fig. 7 | Exploratory tests of model assumptions and network dependence. All panels show single realizations, without replicate-based error bars. a, Emitter-generated leaks at three pressure exponents matched against prescribed-demand (powder blue) and emitter (peach) corpora. Solid bars show partition Top-1; hatched bars show exact-junction accuracy. The dashed line is the prescribed-demand partition control (86.7%). All 60 queries originate at locations represented in both corpora, testing leak-model form and discharge mismatch rather than unseen-location generalization (Supplementary Table S8). b, Recall of the correct resolvable-location class on 200 queries under eleven sensor conditions, comparing projection residual (purple) and trimmed residual (mint). Drift labels give standard deviation in metres. The highlighted combined condition includes drift, missing channels, stuck sensors and spikes; full settings are in Supplementary Table S9. c, True leak classes recovered by exhaustive search (powder blue) and greedy non-negative pursuit (peach) over the 74-class dictionary. Two- and three-leak tests contain 40 scenarios each; the one-leak reference contains 60 and is shared by both solvers. The true number of leaks is supplied (Supplementary Table S10). d, Partition Top-1 on City D and Haining for two 540-template designs: A, 30 locations × 6 magnitudes; B, 180 × 1, each under three demand states. G1/H0 (solid powder blue) retains templates at represented query locations; G1/H2 (hatched powder blue) removes their locations; G2 locations (pale coral) were never represented. Each group contains 180 queries. Absolute accuracies are not comparable across networks (Supplementary Table S11). All 52 estimator–condition values are retained. Source data are provided in the Source Data file.
