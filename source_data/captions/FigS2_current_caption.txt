Fig. S2 | Confusion structure of projection-residual retrieval under H0 (a), H2 (b) and H8 (c), and the fraction of Top-1 errors that fall in adjacent partitions (d). a–c, Rows are true partitions and columns predicted partitions; colours show row-normalized frequencies pooled over the five repetitions of Table S2b (2,700 predictions per protocol; 180 per true partition). Off-diagonal cells with a frequency of at least 0.10 are annotated. Panel titles give the pooled Top-1 accuracy and the pooled fraction of Top-1 errors that fell in a partition adjacent to the true partition (sharing at least one pipe), computed from the summed counts of the five repetitions; these are the values quoted in Supplementary Note 1.7 (206 of 346 errors under H0, 503 of 648 under H2, 825 of 1,086 under H8). d, The same adjacent-error fraction computed separately for each of the five repetitions: bars show the mean of the five per-repetition fractions, black caps ±1 sample s.d. and white circles the individual repetitions (bar fill grades the mean and carries no further information); the value label above each bar gives this mean, and the number of Top-1 errors per repetition (mean ± s.d.), the denominator of each per-repetition fraction, is printed at the base of the bar. Because this denominator differs between repetitions, the mean of the per-repetition fractions differs from the pooled fraction in the titles of a–c by less than 0.3 percentage points. Source data are provided in the Source Data file.
