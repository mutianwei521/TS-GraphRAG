Fig. S3 | Hydraulic and demand mismatch with a frozen nominal dictionary. All 26 prespecified conditions are shown for projection-residual retrieval using the primary day-normal library (hydraulically screened templates; all 30 query junctions excluded). a, Correct and hydraulically admissible queries as a percentage of all 60 planned queries in each realization. Invalid cases remain in the denominator and count as failures. b, Top-1 accuracy conditional on admissible queries; n.a. denotes no admissible queries. c, Mean admissibility rate; the small numbers report the number of seeds with at least one admissible query. Bars in a,b show mean ± sample s.d.; a uses five realizations, whereas b uses only realizations with admissible queries and can have fewer than five. Matched subtracts the no-leak baseline under the same perturbed model and demand; stale subtracts the nominal day-normal baseline. Admissibility requires finite, converged pressures in [0, 200] m at every junction and hourly report, with no hydraulic report warnings. The display includes negative lower SD endpoints; these are not negative probabilities. Negative pump-suction auxiliary-node pressures can trigger this conservative criterion without negative service-node pressures; a separate service-node diagnostic is supplied. Thus reduced success in a can reflect physical invalidity as well as retrieval error. Demand CV denotes the target before total-demand normalization. Perturbation distributions and exact amplitudes are defined in Supplementary Note 1.10. Deterministic control hydraulics are reused with five independent noise realizations; random perturbations vary both hydraulics and noise. These are synthetic tests on daily-average pressure features, not measured field or real-time SCADA performance. All methods, library policies, valid-query counts and raw predictions are supplied in Source Data.
