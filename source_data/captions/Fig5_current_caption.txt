Fig. 5 | Corpus allocation at a fixed 540-template budget. a, Top-1 partition accuracy for locations represented in every corpus (G1, powder blue) and absent from every corpus (G2, pale coral). Designs A, A′, C60, C90 and B allocate locations × magnitudes as labelled, each under three demand states. A′ shares A's locations but excludes query magnitudes; hatching identifies this design. b, Paired G2 Top-1 differences between C90 and A (purple) or B (mint), shown separately for all 15 draws. Dashed lines indicate the paired medians (12.2 and 3.9 percentage points, respectively). Each draw index corresponds to one shared corpus/query-location draw, in seed order 41–55. Legend labels report exact two-sided sign-test P values, Holm-adjusted over all ten design comparisons. C90 exceeds A in all 15 draws (P = 0.00061); it exceeds B in 13 draws, but this comparison remains unresolved at the adjusted 0.05 threshold (P = 0.052). c, G2 Top-3 accuracy and MRR@5 multiplied by 100. d, G2 Euclidean distance from the highest-ranked template location to the true leak, in network-coordinate units. In a, c and d, bars show arithmetic means, charcoal caps ±1 sample standard deviation, and white circles all 15 draw-level observations. Distance summarizes the 15 within-draw medians; its mean is labelled above each bar. All panels use projection-residual retrieval under H0. G1 and G2 contain different query locations, with 180 queries per group per draw. One Gaussian perturbation vector, component standard deviation 0.02 m, is shared across queries within each draw. The budget counts stored templates, not all simulations used for tensor construction. No observations were excluded. Supplementary Table S3 provides the complete comparison family.
