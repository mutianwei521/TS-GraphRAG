Fig. 6 | Physics-aware estimators and architecture components under holdout. Panels a–c compare estimators on all 540 reference queries. a, Top-1 accuracy of all eight estimators under H0–H8. Downward triangles mark the highest observed accuracy within each protocol, including ties, without indicating statistical significance; the dotted line marks chance accuracy, 1/15 (Supplementary Table S5). b, Strict fusion (mint), projection retrieval (hatched lavender) and cosine 1-NN (blue) on identical retained scenarios. Bars give mean Top-1 accuracy and fine caps ±1 sample s.d. over five measurement-noise realizations (540 fixed queries per realization). Labels give fusion minus projection differences calculated before rounding; H2 and H8 are highlighted. All numerical and semantic artifacts are filtered per protocol (Supplementary Table S4). c, Graph-variant minus cosine 1-NN accuracy. The variant combines top-10 rank-weighted votes with one adjacency-smoothing step, whereas cosine 1-NN selects one template; this contrast does not isolate smoothing. Discordant counts, exact McNemar tests and Holm-adjusted P values over eleven comparisons appear in Supplementary Table S6; none of these five contrasts is significant after adjustment. d, Corrections among 120 sampled errors (mint, right) and breakages among 60 sampled correct answers (coral, left) for every language model. Net changes are population-weighted estimates using 126 errors among 540 H2 queries, rather than observed accuracies on all queries. Base accuracy is 76.7%; 57 errors have the truth in the Top-3, giving an 87.2% re-ranking ceiling (Supplementary Table S7). Panels a, c and d retain the historical single realization; only b has replicate-based uncertainty. Projection retrieval excludes zero-response templates throughout. Cosine 1-NN retains 18 such templates in a and c, but excludes them in b (Supplementary Note 1.2). H0 deletes nothing; H1 the exact scenario; H2 location; H3 magnitude; H4 demand state; H5–H7 unions of location/magnitude, location/state and magnitude/state; H8 all three. All evidence uses the unscreened simulated reference corpus. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
